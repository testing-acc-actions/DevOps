How to use your academy

  1. Follow the roadmap in order; do not skip foundations.
  2. Read a concept and explain it in your own words.
  3. Answer questions before opening the answer guidance.
  4. Complete the lab and verify the expected result.
  5. Work through production scenarios using evidence, hypotheses, safe mitigation, root cause and prevention.
  6. Record attendance and notes. Export progress regularly.
  7. Bring your answers, errors or decisions to our sessions for review.
Evaluation approach: I will assess correctness, reasoning, command safety, troubleshooting sequence, security, cost awareness, rollback thinking and clarity. A green pipeline alone is not proof of production readiness.

All progress is stored in this browser's local storage. It is not synced to GitHub or other devices. Export JSON backups periodically.
